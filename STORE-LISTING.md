# Add-on listing (AMO markdown: bold, italic, links, lists)

## Summary (max 250 characters)

Browse and watch Netflix with a game controller. D-pad navigation, an on-screen keyboard for search, playback controls, and button hints that match your Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo pad.

## Description

**Netflix from the couch, with the controller you already own.** Press any button and a highlight ring takes over; you never need the mouse or keyboard again.

- **Browse like a console**: the D-pad moves through rows, titles, menus and search results, and B always brings you back to the card you left
- **TV mode**: the focused title lifts and enlarges while the rest dims, with an info panel and no hover pop-ups
- **Full playback control**: play, seek, volume, skip intro, next episode, subtitles and speed, all from the pad
- **Hints that match your controller**: a bar along the bottom shows Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo icons for what each button does right now

*Runs only on netflix.com, keeps settings in Firefox, sends no data anywhere. Not affiliated with Netflix.*
